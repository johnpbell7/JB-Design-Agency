The Government wants to change how you sell vapes. Tell them what you think.

Protect your sales. Keep your shop running as it is.

The Government is consulting on changes to how vapes and tobacco are sold in shops. Vapes in plain white packs. Flavours cut to a single word. Products kept out of sight and handed over on request. None of it is law yet. It closes on 2 October. If it goes through without the trade having its say, it lands on your shop and your bottom line.

Source: DHSC consultation, published 10 July 2026. These are proposals, not current law.

What this would do to your shop.

Three sets of proposals. None of them is law yet and the wording is still being decided. If they pass as written, they change what you can sell and how you sell it. That is why your answer counts.

Packaging

Every vape in a plain white pack. No brand colours, no imagery and the flavour cut to one word. A shelf where every line looks the same and nothing stands out.

Appearance

Devices in white, black or grey only. No lights, no screens beyond battery level. Every device looks the same - you lose the visual cues that point customers to your best sellers.

Display

Vapes kept out of sight, like tobacco. A customer asks, you fetch it, with a queue behind them. That is your gantry, your impulse sale and a lot more time at the till.

This is our plain-English reading of the published proposals. Check the official consultation for the exact wording before you respond.
