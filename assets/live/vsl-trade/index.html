Before you register.

Can’t see yours? Ask the team.

Is there a minimum order?

Ordering runs through the VSL Trade App and the current terms are shown on your account. Ask your rep or the team and we’ll confirm what applies to your store.

Do I need separate accounts for vape and FMCG?

No. One VSL Trade account covers both — twelve retail categories, one order, one delivery, one conversation if something is wrong.

How quickly do orders arrive?

Orders dispatched before 6pm are delivered the next working day. Delivery coverage varies by area — the team will confirm for your postcode.

Do I get a named contact, or a call centre?

A named rep who knows your store, your area and your range. In store when it matters, on the phone when it doesn’t.

What happens when the vape rules change?

We keep the range, the compliance detail and the display guidance in step, and cover what changed in the News Hub so you aren’t left holding stock you can’t sell.

Is the advice just marketing for your own lines?

The guidance carries its source, owner and date so you can judge it. Anything paid for by a brand is labelled as such, never dressed up as independent advice.

What does it cost to join?

Registering is free and there’s no commitment to order. You only pay for the stock you buy.

Can you help with display and refits?

Yes — display solutions, POS, ranging and in-store execution, for single sites and multi-site estates.
