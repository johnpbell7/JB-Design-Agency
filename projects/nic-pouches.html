The brief

Design and build a nicotine pouch shop where the strength of each pouch is obvious straight away, buying takes fewer steps and people actually notice the Nic Points loyalty scheme.

What I did
  • Five strength levels, each with its own colour
  • Product cards that let you switch strength without leaving the card
  • Quick add to basket, on desktop and on phone
  • Membership cards for the three Nic Points tiers
  • Every page built and working, with real products
