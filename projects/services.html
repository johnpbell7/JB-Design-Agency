Questions people ask.

More answers on the home page, or just ask me.

How much does a website cost?

A one-page website starts from £500, a small business site from £1,200 and a small online shop from £1,800. After a quick chat I send you a fixed quote, so you know the full price before we start.

How long does it take?

Most small business sites take a few weeks from our first chat to going live. Shops and bigger sites take longer, and you’ll get a timeline along with your quote.

Can I update it myself?

It depends on the site. A one-page site works like a shop window, with no logins or back end to look after, which keeps it cheaper, faster and more secure. When you need a change, I make it for you, from £35, or from £40 a month on a care plan. If you’d rather do it yourself, I can add an editor to a one-page site for £150. Bigger sites and shops come with an editor included.

Do you host it and look after it?

Yes, if you’d like me to. Hosting is £10 to £15 a month depending on the site, and keeps it online, secure and backed up. It’s included if you’re on a care plan (from £40 a month), which also covers updates and new pages. Your web address is registered in your name, usually £10 to £20 a year, so it’s always yours. Shopify shops are paid to Shopify directly.

What do you need from me to start?

Just a conversation about your business and what you want the site to do. Your logo, photos and words all help, but if you don’t have them yet, we can sort them out together.
