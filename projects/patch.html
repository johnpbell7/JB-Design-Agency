The brief

A new field-marketing agency working with consumer brands, with no name, logo or anything else yet. It needed the lot, from business cards to a website.

What I did
  • The name, and the strapline Every patch covered
  • A wordmark with a connected A, in a wide sans serif
  • A network pattern that runs off the edge of everything
  • Lime on black, with Proxima Nova for all the type
  • Uniform, print, car wraps, drawings and the website
