Do I need a logo before a website?

It’s one of the first questions new businesses ask. Here’s when a logo should come first, when you can start without one, and why doing both together often works out best.

By John Bell, freelance web and graphic designer in Birmingham · October 2026

The short answer

Ideally, yes, or at least at the same time. Your logo sets the colours, fonts and feel that the website is built around. If you start the site first, you’ll often end up changing it once the logo arrives. Doing both together is usually quicker and cheaper.

Why the logo usually comes first

A website isn’t just your logo in the top corner. Its colours, fonts, buttons and even the style of the photos all come from your brand. When the logo is settled, the website design follows naturally, and everything you make afterwards (signs, cards, social posts) matches.

If the website is designed first, the logo has to fit around it. That often means compromises, or going back and changing the site once you’ve chosen the logo.

When you can start without one

  • You need to be online this week. A simple, clean site with your business name set in a good font is far better than nothing. You can add a logo later.
  • You’re testing an idea. If you’re not sure the business will run, a smart name-only site keeps costs down.
  • Your name is the brand. Consultants, therapists and tradespeople often do well with a well-set name rather than a symbol.

Doing both together

This is what most of my clients choose. We start with the logo, agree it, then design the site around it, all in one project with one person. You avoid paying twice for the same thinking, and everything is ready together.

What to have ready before either

  • Your business name, and how you want it written
  • Two or three businesses whose look you like (in any industry)
  • Who your customers are, and what they should feel when they find you
  • Anything you’re attached to already: a colour, a symbol, an old sign

Questions people ask

Can I add a logo to my website later?

Yes. A logo can always be added later, but if it changes your colours and fonts, parts of the site may need restyling to match.

How much does a logo and website cost together?

A logo and small website together start from £700, which saves £100 on doing them separately.

Can you design a logo from my rough idea?

Yes. Bring a sketch, a photo or just a description. You’ll see a few options, then we refine your favourite with two rounds of amends included.
