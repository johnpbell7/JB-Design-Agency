Print that helps sell new homes.

Print for housebuilders, developers and the estate agents selling for them. I branded new developments, then designed the print to go with each one: brochures, site boards, banners, flyers and adverts.

Work

Developments shown

The brief

Every new development needed a brand of its own and the print to help sell it. The jobs ranged from six homes in Basingstoke to 57 apartments in Hove.

What I did
  • Logos and colours for each development
  • Sales brochures from 4 to 32 pages, with floor plans
  • Site boards and banners you can read from across the road
  • A5 flyers and press adverts that each get one message across
  • One consistent look across every piece
In order

Everything before the show home opens.

  • Logo design

    A mark that sells the place and still works on a site board, a brochure cover and a screen.

  • Property brand look and feel

    Colours, type and photography that make every piece feel like it belongs to the same place.

  • Brochures

    Sales brochures between 4 and 32 pages, carrying the floor plans, specs and maps.

  • Large format printing

    Diamond panels, full site hoardings, banners and boards, all sized to be read from the road.

  • Socials

    Launch posts, adverts and templates for each development, in the same look as the print.

  • Micro websites

    A one-page site for each development, ready to go live on launch day.
