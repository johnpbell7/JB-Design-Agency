The brief

GoSweet sells American sweets, chocolate and fizzy drinks across the UK. I set out to design and build a shop that’s easy to browse and quick to buy from, without losing what makes it fun.

What I did
  • Designed the shop for phones first, then built every page, with a working basket
  • Put the pack price and price per piece on every product card
  • Cut the colours down to purple, pink and a little gold
  • Set the type in Rubik and Plus Jakarta Sans
  • Designed rewards cards and a quicker way to fill the basket
Phone first

Built for shopping on a phone.

I put two products to a row and made the buttons big enough for a thumb, with the basket never more than a tap away.

The logo

A logo with a sweet in it.

I turned the o into a wrapped sweet and kept the rest of the letters round and chunky, so it still reads at small sizes.

Colour + type

A few colours, used carefully.

I kept every button purple, used pink only for savings, and let gold appear just once on each screen.
