Come and rummage.

A one-page website for Beetle Eyes Clothing, Corrine Kaenzig’s vintage rail tucked inside Curious Magpie in Mountsorrel. It opens with a pair of hand-drawn curtains, because a shop like this deserves an entrance.

The brief

There’s no online shop and nothing to check out. The site has one job: make people want to pop in and have a rummage.

What I did
  • Paper, ink and dusty rose, with Fraunces and Inter Tight
  • Hand-drawn curtains that swish open as the page loads
  • A tilted photo collage and a banner of words that scrolls past
  • A scrapbook gallery of taped-up photos
  • Written by hand in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript
