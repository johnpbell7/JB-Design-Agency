A local site for a town that shops local

Sutton is full of independents: salons, cafés, gyms, estate agents, tradespeople and clubs, most of them busy enough that the website gets left until last. A good one quietly does the work for you. It shows up when people nearby search, looks right on a phone, and makes calling or booking the obvious next step.

For Sutton Coldfield Cricket Club I turned decades of scorebooks, photos and stories into a history site that members actually browse. That mix of careful organising and a site people enjoy using is what I bring to every local business. I’m just down the road in Birmingham, so meeting up is easy.

Questions people ask

Do you work with businesses in Sutton Coldfield?

Yes. I designed and built the history website for Sutton Coldfield Cricket Club, and I’m based just down the road in Birmingham, so I’m happy to meet you in Sutton.

How much does a website cost?

A small website starts from £500, five pages or more from £900, ten or more from £1,500, and a small online shop from £1,800. You get a fixed quote after a quick chat, before anything starts.

Can you build a website for our club or society?

Yes. Club sites need fixtures, news, membership details and often a bit of history, all easy for volunteers to keep up to date. The SCCC Heritage site is a good example of what’s possible.

Will people in Sutton find my business on Google?

Every site is quick and set up the way Google expects. I add search pages for the areas you cover where they’ll help, at no extra cost, and help you set up your Google Business Profile so you show on Maps.

Can you do our logo and signage too?

Yes. I design logos and brands from £300, and print such as flyers, signage and banners, with printing arranged if you’d like it.
