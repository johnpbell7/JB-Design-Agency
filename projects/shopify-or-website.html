Shopify or a normal website?

If you sell something, it’s easy to assume you need an online shop. Often you do, but sometimes a normal website with a booking form or a payment link does the job for far less.

By John Bell, freelance web and graphic designer in Birmingham · October 2026

The short answer

If you sell products online, with stock, delivery and lots of orders, use Shopify. If you sell services (appointments, quotes, projects), a normal website with a contact or booking form is simpler and cheaper. Only a few products? A normal site with payment links can be enough to start.

The quick way to decide

If you…You probably need
Sell physical products to the public and post them outShopify
Sell more than about 10 products, or have sizes and coloursShopify
Sell services, appointments or one-off projectsA normal website
Want people to call, book or ask for a quoteA normal website
Sell a handful of products or vouchersA normal website with payment links (start here, move to Shopify later)
Sell to trade customers on accountA trade website with registration, or a Shopify B2B set-up

What Shopify is good at

  • Product pages, stock, sizes and colours, all easy to manage yourself
  • Secure checkout, card payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Delivery rates, discount codes, gift cards and order emails
  • An app on your phone to see and fulfil orders

The trade-off is cost: Shopify charges a monthly fee (from around £25 on its Basic plan; check their current prices) plus card fees on each sale, and paid apps can add up.

What a normal website is good at

  • Explaining what you do and why people should choose you
  • Getting enquiries: calls, quote requests, bookings and WhatsApp messages
  • Showing your work, reviews and prices
  • Low running costs, often just hosting from £10 a month

Start small, grow later

Many businesses start with a normal website and a few payment links, then move to Shopify once orders pick up. Your domain, branding and Google rankings come with you, so nothing is wasted.

Questions people ask

Is Shopify worth it for a small business?

Yes, if you sell products online regularly. For a service business, or only a few products, a normal website is usually cheaper and simpler.

Can I take payments on a normal website?

Yes. Payment links or a simple checkout can take card payments for a few products, deposits or vouchers without a full shop.

Can you move my shop to Shopify?

Yes. I can design and set up a new Shopify shop or refresh an existing one, and help move your products across.
