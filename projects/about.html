The story so far

How I got here.

  2. 01 · Uni

    It started at Nottingham Trent.

    I graduated with First Class Honours, then found my feet in a design agency.

  3. 02 · Agency

    Then agency life.

    I ran jobs from start to finish and worked with clients directly. That meant reports and films for a law firm, 5G explainers, a product launch, charity reports and property marketing.

  4. 03 · In-house

    Then an in-house brand team.

    Next I joined a team looking after a group of consumer brands. I designed logos, online shops and a trade website, and created a whole new agency brand, name and all.

  5. 04 · Now

    Now I work for myself.

    After five years in full-time jobs, I’ve gone freelance. I work to the same standards, but now you talk straight to the person designing and building your site.
