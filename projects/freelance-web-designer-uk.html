Questions people ask

Can you work with me if I’m not in Birmingham?

Yes. I’m based in Birmingham, but my projects run over video calls and email, so it makes no difference where in the UK your business is.

How do remote projects work?

We start with a video call, then I send you a fixed quote. I share the designs on screen and by link so you can see them on your own laptop and phone, and we keep in touch by email while I build. Nothing goes live until you’re happy with it.

Do we ever meet in person?

Usually there’s no need, as video calls and shared screens cover everything from the first chat to the final checks. If you’re within reach of Birmingham and would like to meet, I’m happy to.

How much does it cost?

Websites start from £500 for a small site, £900 for five pages or more and £1,500 for ten or more, and small online shops from £1,800. Logos start from £300, brand identities from £500 and print from £45 a page. You get a fixed quote before we start.

How long does it take?

A small website usually takes about a week from our first call to going live, a medium site two to three weeks, and larger sites and shops four weeks or more. You’ll get a timeline with your quote, and a fast turnaround can be requested.
