Questions people ask

Do you work with businesses in Solihull?

Yes. I’m based in Birmingham, a short drive from Solihull, and happy to meet you locally. Everything else runs over calls and email, and it’s me you deal with throughout.

How much does a website cost?

A small website starts from £500, five pages or more from £900, ten or more from £1,500, and a small online shop from £1,800. You get a fixed quote before we start.

Can you design our stand or banners for the NEC?

Yes. I design roller banners, stand graphics, flyers and price lists that match your brand, and can arrange the printing. Large format starts from £80, priced on size and number of panels.

I’m a wedding supplier. Can you help?

Yes. I designed the website and brand for Birdie Blooms, a flower studio that does weddings and events, so I know how much the photos, enquiries and wedding-fair print matter.

Will my site show up when people search in Solihull?

Every site is quick and set up the way Google expects, with search pages for the areas you cover where they’ll help, and help setting up your Google Business Profile.
