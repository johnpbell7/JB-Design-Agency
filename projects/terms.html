Terms, in plain English.

How this website can be used, and how quotes, changes, payment and ownership work when we work together.

Last updated 10 October 2026

This page covers two things: using this website, and how I work when you hire me. Your written quote sets out the details for your project, and if it says something different from this page, the quote wins.

Using this website

This website belongs to John Bell, a freelance graphic and web designer based in Birmingham, UK. By using it, you agree to these terms.

  • The work shown. The designs, websites and images here are shown as examples of my work. Client names, logos and products belong to their owners. Some projects, such as GoSweet and Nic Pouches, show my own full designs and working builds. They aren’t the businesses’ live websites.

Working together

Quotes

After we’ve talked about your project, I’ll send you a written quote with the price, what’s included and a rough timeline. The price is fixed for the work described. Work starts once you’ve accepted the quote and paid any deposit it asks for.

Changes and extra work

Each quote includes a set number of rounds of changes. If you’d like something that wasn’t in the quote, or more changes than it includes, I’ll tell you the cost before I do it, so there are no surprises.

What I need from you

You’ll provide the words, photos, logos and other content for your project, and confirm you have the right to use them. If content arrives late, the timeline may move to match.

Payment

Invoices are payable within the time stated on them. I may pause work, or hold off launching or handing over files, until overdue invoices are paid.

Who owns the work

Once you’ve paid in full, you own the final design and the content made for you. Fonts, stock photos, plugins and other third-party items stay under their own licences, which I’ll arrange in your name or tell you about. I may show the finished work in my portfolio unless you ask me not to.

Hosting and other services

Domains, hosting, email and similar services are run by other companies and are billed to you, either by them directly or passed on at cost. I’m not responsible for outages or changes on their side, but I’ll always help you sort problems out.

If plans change

You can stop a project at any time by letting me know in writing. You’ll pay for the work done up to that point, and I’ll hand over everything finished so far once that’s paid.

Liability

I take care with everything I make and test it before it goes live. If something goes wrong because of my work, I’ll put it right. My total liability for any project is limited to the amount you’ve paid me for it. Nothing here limits any rights you have by law that can’t be limited.

The law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
