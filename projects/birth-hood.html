The brief

Leanne needed a warm site she could edit herself. It also had to answer the thing she gets asked most, which is which doula package is the right one.

What I did
  • Pink colours, Poppins type and Leanne’s handwritten sign-off
  • Sticker-style cards with scalloped edges, made in code
  • A collage of polaroid photos at the top of the home page
  • A five-question quiz to find the right package
  • Built in Next.js, with Sanity for editing and Stripe for payments
