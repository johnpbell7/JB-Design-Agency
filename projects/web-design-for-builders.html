Word of mouth gets you so far

Most trades run on recommendations and a Facebook page, and that works, until someone new searches “builder near me” or “kitchen fitter Tamworth” and finds the firm down the road instead.

A good trade website does three jobs. It shows your work properly, so people trust you before they call. It turns up when people search for the work you do in the towns you cover. And it makes asking for a quote so easy that they do it there and then, on their phone, without hunting for your number.

You won’t need to learn anything new. Send me your photos and a few lines about your jobs, and I’ll do the rest.

What a trade website needs

A quote form that goes to your WhatsApp

Name, postcode, type of work and the job, written out for you. The customer adds photos and presses send, and it lands where you already are.

Your number one tap away

A call button that follows people down the page on their phone, because most of your customers will find you on one.

Your jobs, in real photos

Each finished job with its own photos and before and afters. Real work wins jobs, and Google rewards it too.

A page for each service

Kitchens, extensions, loft conversions, carpentry: each with its own page, so you show up when people search for that job.

A page for each town you cover

Lichfield, Tamworth, Sutton Coldfield, wherever you work. Each page written for that town, not copied and pasted.

Help getting on the map

I’ll help you set up your Google Business Profile, so you show on Google Maps next to your website.

Questions trades ask

Do I need a website if I have a Facebook page?

Facebook is great for people who already follow you. A website is what Google shows a stranger searching for “builder in Lichfield” at nine at night, and it’s yours: your jobs, your prices and your quote form, with nothing else in the way. Keep posting on Facebook and link each job back to your site.

How much does a website for a builder cost?

A small site starts from £500, up to ten pages from £900, and eleven pages or more from £1,500. If you need a logo too, a logo and small website together start from £700. Hosting is from £10 a month. You get a fixed quote before we start.

How do quote requests reach me?

However suits you. The form can open WhatsApp with the customer’s name, postcode, type of work and job details already written out, so they just add photos and press send. Or it can come to you by email. Either way there’s no new inbox to check.

Can I add new jobs myself?

Send me the photos and a line or two about each job and I’ll add it, with its own page if there’s enough to show. That’s covered by a care plan from £40 a month, or quoted per job.

Will it get me on Google?

Each service and each town you cover gets its own page, set up the way Google expects, and I’ll help you set up your Google Business Profile, which puts you on the map. Be ready for it to take a few months: a new website climbs over time, and reviews from your customers speed it up.

How long does it take?

A small website usually takes about a week from our first chat to going live, and a site with a page for each service and town two to three weeks. Most of the wait is usually photos, so dig out your best before and afters.

Ready for the phone to ring?

Tell me what you do and where you work, and send a link to your Facebook page if you have one. I usually reply within a working day.
