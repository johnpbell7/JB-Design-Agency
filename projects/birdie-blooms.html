The brief

A website for a one-woman flower studio that works properly on a phone, and then a logo to go with it. There were no studio photos to work with yet, and most of her orders start on Instagram.

What I did
  • Four colour schemes that switch over with the seasons
  • Newsreader for headings, with Josefin Sans for the small labels
  • A Birdie Card for collecting stamps
  • Six ink drawings that draw themselves onto the page
  • Eight logo ideas, whittled down to two
Branding · in progress

Eight logo ideas for Birdie Blooms.

Rather than send eight logos on a white page, I put each one on her actual website so she could judge them where they’d really be used. We’re down to two, and the choice is with her now.
