The brief

Somewhere to keep over 180 years of club archive, from team photos to scorebooks and minute books, that members can read on a phone.

What I did
  • A slider through the decades, with a cricket ball as the handle
  • A home page that opens when you bowl the ball
  • Roll of honour cards for 39 members
  • Archivo Black for headings, Georgia for the long stories
  • Built in React, Vite and Tailwind
On a phone

Club history you can read on your phone.

The long stories are in big, easy-to-read type, every photo has a caption, and the menu is always close by.
