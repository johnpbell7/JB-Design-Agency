Questions people ask

Do you work with businesses in Coventry?

Yes. I’m based in Birmingham and work across Coventry and Warwickshire, meeting in person if you’d like or over video calls.

Can you build a website for a start-up?

Yes. I can design your name, logo and launch site, then grow it as you go. I did exactly that for What’s in my Fridge, my own web app, from the first landing page to the live app.

How much does a website cost?

A small website starts from £500, five pages or more from £900, ten or more from £1,500, and a small online shop from £1,800. Web apps are quoted to suit. You get a fixed quote before we start.

Can you build an app, not just a website?

Yes, for simple web apps that run in the browser and on phones, with sign-up and payments. Tell me the idea and I’ll tell you honestly what it would take.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site is quick and set up the way Google expects, with search pages for what you do and where where they’ll help, and help with your Google Business Profile.
